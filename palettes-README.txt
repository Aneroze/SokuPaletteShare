Your own palettes go here, in the folder of their character:

    palettes\<character>\

reimu, marisa, sakuya, alice, patchouli, youmu, remilia, yuyuko, yukari, suika, udonge (Reisen),
aya, komachi, iku, tenshi, sanae, chirno (Cirno), meirin (Meiling), utsuho (Okuu), suwako.

Files: .pal, .act or .bmp; the file name is the palette's name in the picker. New files show up the
next time you get to that character's palette step. In game: Profile > Deck Construction >
a character > C > "Open palette folder" opens the character's folder.

Backup: PaletteShare keeps a copy of this folder in <game folder>\PaletteShare\backup. Removing the
mod (SokuLauncher deletes this whole mod folder) doesn't lose your palettes: install it again and
they come back. Palettes you delete here are moved to backup\_removed there, never deleted. To get
rid of them for good, delete <game folder>\PaletteShare\backup too.
